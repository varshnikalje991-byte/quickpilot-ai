# 🚀 QuickPilot AI

### Capture anything. Get your day organized.

**QuickPilot AI** is an AI-powered productivity app designed to help you capture ideas, organize tasks, manage notes, plan your day, and keep track of reminders — all in one place.

The goal is simple: spend less time organizing your life and more time getting things done.

## ✨ Features

- 📝 **Quick Capture** — Quickly save ideas, thoughts, and important information.
- ✅ **Smart Task Management** — Organize tasks and keep track of what needs to be done.
- 🧠 **AI-Powered Organization** — Help categorize captured information and turn it into actionable items, where supported.
- ⏰ **Reminders** — Keep important tasks and deadlines on your radar.
- 📅 **Daily Planning** — Plan your day and prioritize your activities.
- 📚 **Notes & Ideas** — Keep useful information organized in one place.
- 🔎 **Easy Search** — Find your saved information quickly.
- 📱 **Mobile-Friendly Design** — Designed with convenient access across screen sizes in mind.

*Feature availability depends on the current implementation of the app.*

## 🎯 Our Mission

QuickPilot AI aims to make everyday productivity simpler by bringing capturing, organizing, and planning together in one convenient experience.
